THE PROJECT

A small tool for a specific kind of backlog.

Everybody Codes Solver was started to help the TLV team work through Everybody Codes problems that are out of date—older than five days. The aim is simple: make it easier to bring a challenge into a clear, focused solving space.

This site is the lightweight starting point. The solution page provides a reusable input-and-output layout; a challenge-solving implementation can be connected when one is available.
